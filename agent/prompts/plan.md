Plan: $@.

Read `diffing-plan-review`. Inspect the relevant code, then submit a concise plan with
scope, changes, verification, and risks. Pass it inline or keep its source under
`~/.diffing/`, never in the consumer tree. Print the plan URL and park for review.
Only approval authorizes implementation; follow the skill for revisions and other verdicts.
The lead owns the plan. This explicit command requests review even for a small task.
